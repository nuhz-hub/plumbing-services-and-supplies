# 🚰 Plumbing Services & Supplies

## Overview
Plumbing Services & Supplies is a web-based platform designed to connect customers with professional plumbers while also providing access to essential plumbing materials. The project combines **service booking** and **supply catalog** features into one solution, making it easier for users to request help and purchase parts.

---

## ✨ Business Viable Product (BVP) Features

### 🔧 Core Services
- **Leak Repair** – Quick response to fix water leaks.
- **WC & Shower Installation** – Professional bathroom fittings and installations.
- **Pipe Replacement** – Durable materials for long-lasting plumbing solutions.
- **Plumbing Supplies for Sale** – Access to quality parts, fittings, and tools.

### 🌟 Value Proposition
- **Professional Team** – Skilled plumbers with hands-on expertise.
- **High-Quality Materials** – Ensures reliability and durability.
- **Affordable Pricing** – Competitive rates for services and supplies.
- **Fast & Reliable Service** – Quick turnaround for urgent needs.

### 📞 Contact & Accessibility
- **Phone Support** – Direct line for service requests.
- **WhatsApp Integration** – Easy chat and booking.
- **Email Support** – For inquiries and follow-ups.
- **Location-Based Service** – Operating in FCT Abuja, Nigeria.

## 🚀 How It Works
1. Customers visit the platform and submit a **service request form**.
2. Requests are matched with available plumbers.
3. Customers can browse the **supplies catalog** for parts and tools.
4. Confirmation is sent via phone, WhatsApp, or email.
